K. Maragatham Kumaravel of TVK won the Madurantakam Assembly by-election (Chengalpattu district, reserved for Scheduled Castes), taking 39.96% of the 1,85,292 votes polled. The margin over I. Paranthamen (DMK) was 20,040 votes, or 10.82% of votes polled. Polling was on 6 October 2026 and the votes were counted on 9 October 2026.

## Why Madurantakam went to a by-election

In the May 2026 general election the AIADMK's K. Maragatham Kumaravel won Madurantakam by 7,194 votes (3.63%). On 25 May 2026 the MLA resigned and joined TVK, which fielded K. Maragatham Kumaravel again in the same seat. The defecting MLA won the seat back on TVK's ticket. The AIADMK finished third.
