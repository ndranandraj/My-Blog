Vijay. M of TVK won the Usilampatti Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 29.34% of 224,048 votes cast. The winning margin over Mahendran. I (ADMK) was 1,805 votes, or 0.81 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
