Jayakumar M. of DMK won the Udumalaipettai Assembly constituency (Tiruppur district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.67% of 203,575 votes cast. The winning margin over Radhakrishnan K. (ADMK) was 2,882 votes, or 1.42 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
