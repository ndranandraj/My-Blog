Palanivel. M.S of TVK won the Veerapandi Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.88% of 235,875 votes cast. The winning margin over Sri Balaji Sugumar. S (ADMK) was 4,071 votes, or 1.73 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
