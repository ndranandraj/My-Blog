Thahira of TVK won the Ranipet Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.24% of 221,038 votes cast. The winning margin over R. Gandhi (DMK) was 5,787 votes, or 2.62 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
