Venkataramanan. P of TVK won the Mylapore Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 46.53% of 150,594 votes cast. The winning margin over Velu. Dha (DMK) was 28,972 votes, or 19.24 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
