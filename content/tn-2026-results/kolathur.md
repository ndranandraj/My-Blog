V. S. Babu of TVK won the Kolathur Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.09% of 184,051 votes cast. The winning margin over M. K. Stalin (DMK) was 8,795 votes, or 4.78 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
