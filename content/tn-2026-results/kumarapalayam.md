C.Vijayalakshmi of TVK won the Kumarapalayam Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.54% of 205,324 votes cast. The winning margin over P.Thangamani (ADMK) was 7,696 votes, or 3.75 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
