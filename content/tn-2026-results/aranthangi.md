Mohamed Farvas. J of TVK won the Aranthangi Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.08% of 182,730 votes cast. The winning margin over Ramachandran . T (INC) was 10,062 votes, or 5.51 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
