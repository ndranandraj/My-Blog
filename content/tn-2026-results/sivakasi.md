Keerthana S of TVK won the Sivakasi Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.51% of 193,517 votes cast. The winning margin over Ashokan G (INC) was 11,670 votes, or 6.03 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
