Arunmozhithevan . A of ADMK won the Bhuvanagiri Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.52% of 207,310 votes cast. The winning margin over Saravanan. Durai . K (DMK) was 2,487 votes, or 1.2 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
