Praveen T.T of INC won the Vilavancode Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.45% of 174,927 votes cast. The winning margin over Michael Kumar K (TVK) was 20,970 votes, or 11.99 percentage points. INC held the seat it won here in 2021.
