K. Nithyanandhan of DMK won the Pollachi Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.22% of 181,196 votes cast. The winning margin over Pollachi V. Jayaraman (ADMK) was 4,627 votes, or 2.55 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
