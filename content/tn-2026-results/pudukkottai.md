V. Muthuraja of DMK won the Pudukkottai Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.1% of 190,378 votes cast. The winning margin over K.M. Shariff (TVK) was 1,867 votes, or 0.98 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
