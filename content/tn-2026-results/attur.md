Jayasankaran. A.P. of ADMK won the Attur Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.81% of 208,296 votes cast. The winning margin over Selvabharathi. R. (TVK) was 15,318 votes, or 7.35 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
