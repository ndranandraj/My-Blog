Vignesh K of TVK won the Kinathukadavu Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.31% of 267,917 votes cast. The winning margin over Sabari Karthikeyan K.V.K.S (DMK) was 11,710 votes, or 4.37 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
