Ramachandran. T of CPI won the Thalli Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.02% of 205,878 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr.Nagesh Kumar. C (BJP) was 5,240 votes, or 2.55 percentage points. CPI held the seat it won here in 2021.
