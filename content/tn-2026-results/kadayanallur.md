Rajendran. T. M of DMK won the Kadayanallur Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.97% of 221,951 votes cast. The winning margin over C. Krishnamurali (ADMK) was 6,253 votes, or 2.82 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
