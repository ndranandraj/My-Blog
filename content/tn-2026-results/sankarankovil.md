Dr. Dhilipan Jaishankar of ADMK won the Sankarankovil Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.01% of 196,480 votes cast. The winning margin over C. Ramarajan (TVK) was 6,489 votes, or 3.3 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
