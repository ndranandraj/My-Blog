D.Shanmugan of TVK won the Modakkurichi Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.83% of 190,759 votes cast. The winning margin over S.Kirthika (BJP) was 2,430 votes, or 1.27 percentage points. The seat **flipped from BJP to TVK** since 2021.
