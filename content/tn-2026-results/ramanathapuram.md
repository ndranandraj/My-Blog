Katharbatcha Muthuramalingam of DMK won the Ramanathapuram Assembly constituency (Ramanathapuram district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.52% of 237,576 votes cast. The winning margin over Shahul Hameed (TVK) was 12,459 votes, or 5.24 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
