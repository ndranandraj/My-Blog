J.Kamatchi of TVK won the Pallavaram Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 47.1% of 283,677 votes cast. The winning margin over D.Murugesan (DMDK) was 54,693 votes, or 19.28 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
