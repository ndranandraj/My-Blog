Sathya. M of TVK won the Krishnarayapuram Assembly constituency (Karur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.94% of 189,357 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. Dhivya. S (ADMK) was 3,503 votes, or 1.85 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
