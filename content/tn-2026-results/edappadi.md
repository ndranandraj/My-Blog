Edappadi Palaniswami. K of ADMK won the Edappadi Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 57.67% of 258,263 votes cast. The winning margin over Premkumar. K (IND) was 98,110 votes, or 37.99 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
