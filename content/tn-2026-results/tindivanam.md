Vanni Arasu of VCK won the Tindivanam Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.56% of 196,026 votes cast. The winning margin over Arjunan. P (ADMK) was 734 votes, or 0.37 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to VCK** since 2021.
