Haribaskar.P of ADMK won the Anthiyur Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.32% of 185,775 votes cast. The winning margin over Sivabalan.M (DMK) was 1,260 votes, or 0.68 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
