M.Vignesh of TVK won the Musiri Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.69% of 189,141 votes cast. The winning margin over N.S.Karunairaaja (DMK) was 17,442 votes, or 9.22 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
