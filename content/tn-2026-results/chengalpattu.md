S. Thiyagarajan of TVK won the Chengalpattu Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.32% of 324,010 votes cast. The winning margin over Karthikdhandapani (DMK) was 35,641 votes, or 11.0 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
