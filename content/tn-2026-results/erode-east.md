M.Vijay Balaji of TVK won the Erode (East) Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.93% of 162,453 votes cast. The winning margin over Gopinath Palaniyappan (INC) was 23,966 votes, or 14.75 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
