N Elaiyaraja of TVK won the Uthangarai Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.16% of 205,530 votes cast. The winning margin over T M Tamilselvam (ADMK) was 5,198 votes, or 2.53 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
