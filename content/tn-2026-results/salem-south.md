Vijay Tamilan Parthiban. A of TVK won the Salem (South) Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.95% of 207,902 votes cast. The winning margin over Loganathan. M (DMK) was 33,369 votes, or 16.05 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
