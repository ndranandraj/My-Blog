Vaithilingam G. of PMK won the Jayankondam Assembly constituency (Ariyalur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.94% of 228,548 votes cast. The winning margin over Kannan Ka.So.Ka. (DMK) was 18,490 votes, or 8.09 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to PMK** since 2021.
