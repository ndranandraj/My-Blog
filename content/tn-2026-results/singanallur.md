K.S.Sri Giri Prasath of TVK won the Singanallur Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.08% of 221,033 votes cast. The winning margin over V.Srinidhi (INC) was 19,139 votes, or 8.66 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
