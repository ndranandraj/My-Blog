Sivakumar. K of TVK won the Perambalur Assembly constituency (Perambalur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.83% of 253,654 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. Jayalakshmi. S.T (DMK) was 14,393 votes, or 5.67 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
