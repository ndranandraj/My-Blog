Sabari Iyngaran G. of TVK won the Periyakulam Assembly constituency (Theni district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.56% of 211,209 votes cast. The winning margin over Sakthivel P. (VCK) was 19,321 votes, or 9.15 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
