Balamurugan. S of TVK won the Tiruppur (South) Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.04% of 179,814 votes cast. The winning margin over Dineshkumar. N (DMK) was 12,901 votes, or 7.17 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
