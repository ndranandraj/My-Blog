Ambethkumar. S of DMK won the Vandavasi Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.37% of 191,204 votes cast. The winning margin over Rani. P (ADMK) was 3,333 votes, or 1.74 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
