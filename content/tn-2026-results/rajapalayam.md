Jegadeshwari. K of TVK won the Rajapalayam Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.81% of 183,020 votes cast. The winning margin over Thangapandian. S (DMK) was 10,605 votes, or 5.79 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
