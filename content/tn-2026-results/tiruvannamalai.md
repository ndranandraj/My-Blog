Velu. E.V of DMK won the Tiruvannamalai Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.11% of 220,066 votes cast. The winning margin over Arul Arumugam (TVK) was 2,455 votes, or 1.12 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
