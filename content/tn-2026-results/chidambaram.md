Thamimun Ansari. M of DMK won the Chidambaram Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.48% of 196,586 votes cast. The winning margin over Pandian. K.A (ADMK) was 5,747 votes, or 2.92 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
