Panneerselvam.O of DMK won the Bodinayakanur Assembly constituency (Theni district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.62% of 220,628 votes cast. The winning margin over Prakash.S (TVK) was 6,805 votes, or 3.08 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
