Jamal Mohamed Younoos. Y.N of INC won the Mayiladuthurai Assembly constituency (Nagapattinam district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.09% of 188,461 votes cast. The winning margin over Palanisamy. A M.Com. (PMK) was 10,845 votes, or 5.75 percentage points. INC held the seat it won here in 2021.
