Saravanan. G of TVK won the Srivaikuntam Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.34% of 176,421 votes cast. The winning margin over Shanmuganathan. S. P (ADMK) was 1,186 votes, or 0.67 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
