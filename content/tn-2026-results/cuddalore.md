B.Rajkumar of TVK won the Cuddalore Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.22% of 190,378 votes cast. The winning margin over A.S.Chandarasekaran (INC) was 15,519 votes, or 8.15 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
