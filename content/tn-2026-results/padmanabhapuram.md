Chellaswamy. R of CPI(M) won the Padmanabhapuram Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.18% of 180,555 votes cast. The winning margin over Krishna Kumar. S (TVK) was 15,569 votes, or 8.62 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to CPI(M)** since 2021.
