Senthilkumar. I.P of DMK won the Dindigul Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.13% of 212,024 votes cast. The winning margin over Nazeer Raja. G (TVK) was 1,131 votes, or 0.53 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
