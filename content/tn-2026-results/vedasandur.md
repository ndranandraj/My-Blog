Saminathan. T of DMK won the Vedasandur Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.5% of 226,554 votes cast. The winning margin over Paramasivam. V.P.B. (ADMK) was 10,063 votes, or 4.44 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
