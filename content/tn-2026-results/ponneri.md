Dr.Ravi.M.S of TVK won the Ponneri Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 48.69% of 226,819 votes cast. The winning margin over Durai Chandrasekar (INC) was 55,768 votes, or 24.59 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
