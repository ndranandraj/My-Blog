Udhayanidhi Stalin of DMK won the Chepauk-Thiruvallikeni Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.76% of 140,741 votes cast. The winning margin over Selvam. D (TVK) was 7,140 votes, or 5.07 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
