M.R. Vijayabhaskar of ADMK won the Karur Assembly constituency (Karur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.99% of 216,829 votes cast. The winning margin over Mathiyalagan V.P (TVK) was 1,821 votes, or 0.84 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
