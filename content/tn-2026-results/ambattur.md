Balamurugan.G of TVK won the Ambattur Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 51.29% of 259,986 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr A.P. Poornima (DMK) was 58,781 votes, or 22.61 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
