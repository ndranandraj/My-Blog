R Jayaramakrishnan of DMK won the Madathukulam Assembly constituency (Tiruppur district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.43% of 188,249 votes cast. The winning margin over C Shanmugavelu (AMMK) was 15,968 votes, or 8.48 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
