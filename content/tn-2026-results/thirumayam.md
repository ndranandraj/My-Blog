Regupathy.S of DMK won the Thirumayam Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.87% of 182,612 votes cast. The winning margin over Chinthamani.C (TVK) was 1,492 votes, or 0.82 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
