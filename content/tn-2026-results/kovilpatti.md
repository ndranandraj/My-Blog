Karunanithi.K of DMK won the Kovilpatti Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.74% of 194,232 votes cast. The winning margin over Balasubramanian.S (TVK) was 843 votes, or 0.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
