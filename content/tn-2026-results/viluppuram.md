Lakshmanan R of DMK won the Viluppuram Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.88% of 215,422 votes cast. The winning margin over Mohanraj N (TVK) was 4,119 votes, or 1.91 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
