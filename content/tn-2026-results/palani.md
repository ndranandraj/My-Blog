Ravimanoharan. K of ADMK won the Palani Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.11% of 208,619 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. Praveen Kumar. M (TVK) was 693 votes, or 0.33 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
