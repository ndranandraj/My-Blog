Rajeev of TVK won the Tiruvadanai Assembly constituency (Ramanathapuram district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.57% of 220,278 votes cast. The winning margin over Rm. Karumanickam (INC) was 2,513 votes, or 1.14 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
