Mukundhan.P of TVK won the Krishnagiri Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.83% of 230,181 votes cast. The winning margin over Ashokkumar.K (ADMK) was 18,844 votes, or 8.19 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
