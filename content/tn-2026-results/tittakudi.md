Ganesan C.V of DMK won the Tittakudi Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.91% of 175,754 votes cast. The winning margin over Rajasekar A (TVK) was 2,629 votes, or 1.5 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
