M.M.Vinoth Kannan of TVK won the Vellore Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.33% of 190,527 votes cast. The winning margin over P.Karthikeyan (DMK) was 6,777 votes, or 3.56 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
