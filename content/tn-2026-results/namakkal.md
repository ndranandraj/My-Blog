Dilip C S of TVK won the Namakkal Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.56% of 212,283 votes cast. The winning margin over Sridevi Mohan P S (ADMK) was 11,008 votes, or 5.19 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
