Annadurai K of DMK won the Pattukkottai Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.9% of 183,745 votes cast. The winning margin over Mathan C (TVK) was 13,754 votes, or 7.49 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
