Anitha R. Radhakrishnan of DMK won the Tiruchendur Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.09% of 186,050 votes cast. The winning margin over J. Murugan (TVK) was 5,872 votes, or 3.16 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
