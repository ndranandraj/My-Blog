Kamaraj. R of ADMK won the Nannilam Assembly constituency (Thiruvarur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.7% of 231,444 votes cast. The winning margin over Mohamed Mubarak (DMK) was 41,724 votes, or 18.03 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
