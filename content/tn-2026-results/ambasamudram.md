Dr.Esakki Subaya of ADMK won the Ambasamudram Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.3% of 191,195 votes cast. The winning margin over V.P.Durai (INC) was 10,245 votes, or 5.36 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
