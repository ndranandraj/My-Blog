Rhevanth Charan of TVK won the Maduravoyal Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 48.26% of 293,692 votes cast. The winning margin over Ganapathy. K (DMK) was 61,509 votes, or 20.94 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
