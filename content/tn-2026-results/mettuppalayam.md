Sunilanand of TVK won the Mettuppalayam Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 30.97% of 244,319 votes cast. The winning margin over Smt.Kavitha Kalyanasundaram (DMK) was 7,768 votes, or 3.18 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
