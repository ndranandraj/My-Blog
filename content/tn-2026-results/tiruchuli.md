Thangam Thenarasu of DMK won the Tiruchuli Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.43% of 181,250 votes cast. The winning margin over Samayan.S (TVK) was 13,485 votes, or 7.44 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
