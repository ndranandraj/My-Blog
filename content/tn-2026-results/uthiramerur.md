Munirathinam.J of TVK won the Uthiramerur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.28% of 227,765 votes cast. The winning margin over Sundar.K (DMK) was 14,223 votes, or 6.24 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
