K.V. Vijay Damu of TVK won the Royapuram Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 46.4% of 127,356 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. A. Subair Khan (DMK) was 14,249 votes, or 11.19 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
