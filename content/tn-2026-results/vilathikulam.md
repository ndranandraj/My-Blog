Markandayan G V of DMK won the Vilathikulam Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.4% of 174,814 votes cast. The winning margin over P. Kasiram (TVK) was 8,228 votes, or 4.71 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
