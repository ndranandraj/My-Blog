Kumar. R of TVK won the Velachery Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.52% of 184,815 votes cast. The winning margin over Ashok. M.K (ADMK) was 33,305 votes, or 18.02 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
