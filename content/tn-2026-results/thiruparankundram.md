Nirmalkumar. R. of TVK won the Thiruparankundram Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.75% of 255,466 votes cast. The winning margin over Kiruthiga Thangapandi (DMK) was 41,553 votes, or 16.27 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
