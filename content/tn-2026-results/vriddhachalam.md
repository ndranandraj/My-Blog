Premallatha Vijayakant of DMDK won the Vriddhachalam Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.15% of 209,186 votes cast. The winning margin over Vijay.S (TVK) was 2,387 votes, or 1.14 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to DMDK** since 2021.
