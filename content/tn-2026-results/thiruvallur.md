Dr. T. Arunkumar of TVK won the Thiruvallur Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.71% of 226,428 votes cast. The winning margin over V.G. Raajendran (DMK) was 24,760 votes, or 10.94 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
