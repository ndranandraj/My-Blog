G.Hari of ADMK won the Tiruttani Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.34% of 238,798 votes cast. The winning margin over M.Sathya Kumar (TVK) was 5,793 votes, or 2.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
