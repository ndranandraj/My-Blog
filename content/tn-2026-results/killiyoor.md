Rajesh Kumar. S of INC won the Killiyoor Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.42% of 177,534 votes cast. The winning margin over Sabin. S (TVK) was 1,311 votes, or 0.74 percentage points. INC held the seat it won here in 2021.
