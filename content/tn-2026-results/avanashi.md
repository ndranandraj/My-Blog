Kamali.S of TVK won the Avanashi Assembly constituency (Tiruppur district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.36% of 231,600 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. L. Murugan (BJP) was 15,373 votes, or 6.64 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
