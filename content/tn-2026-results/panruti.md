Mohan. K of ADMK won the Panruti Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.2% of 210,767 votes cast. The winning margin over Abdur Rahman. A.R (VCK) was 10,663 votes, or 5.06 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
