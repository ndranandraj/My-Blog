Arunraj K G of TVK won the Tiruchengodu Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.6% of 191,122 votes cast. The winning margin over Srmt Sekar (A) Chandrasekar R (ADMK) was 28,172 votes, or 14.74 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
