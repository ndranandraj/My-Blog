R. Vaithilingam of DMK won the Orathanadu Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.42% of 195,304 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. K. Arvind (TVK) was 35,028 votes, or 17.94 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
