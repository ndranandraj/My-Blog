Ananth Moghan K.K. of TVK won the Erode (West) Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.74% of 221,400 votes cast. The winning margin over S. Muthusamy (DMK) was 22,250 votes, or 10.05 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
