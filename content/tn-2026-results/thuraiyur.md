Ravisankar.M of TVK won the Thuraiyur Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.93% of 184,426 votes cast. The winning margin over E.Saroja (ADMK) was 9,614 votes, or 5.21 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
