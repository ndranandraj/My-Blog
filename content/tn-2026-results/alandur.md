M.Harish of TVK won the Alandur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.9% of 261,570 votes cast. The winning margin over T.M.Anbarasan (DMK) was 29,609 votes, or 11.32 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
