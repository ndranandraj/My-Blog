P Chandrasekar of TVK won the Senthamangalam Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.82% of 209,687 votes cast. The winning margin over C Chandrasekaran (ADMK) was 2,655 votes, or 1.27 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
