Maragatham Vetrivel of ADMK won the Pappireddipatti Assembly constituency (Dharmapuri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.03% of 236,621 votes cast. The winning margin over Palaniappan. P (DMK) was 33,114 votes, or 13.99 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
