Kamaraj. S of AMMK won the Mannargudi Assembly constituency (Thiruvarur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.25% of 194,100 votes cast. The winning margin over Rajaa.T.R.B (DMK) was 1,566 votes, or 0.81 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to Amma Makkal Munnettra Kazagam** since 2021.
