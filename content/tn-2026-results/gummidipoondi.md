S.Vijayakumar of TVK won the Gummidipoondi Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.56% of 232,522 votes cast. The winning margin over Sudhakar.V (ADMK) was 27,945 votes, or 12.02 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
