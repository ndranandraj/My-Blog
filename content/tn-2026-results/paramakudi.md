Advocate. Kathiravan. K.K of DMK won the Paramakudi Assembly constituency (Ramanathapuram district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.18% of 189,753 votes cast. The winning margin over Gopirajan. G (TVK) was 3,548 votes, or 1.87 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
