Dr M Sudhakar of TVK won the Katpadi Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.33% of 203,497 votes cast. The winning margin over V. Ramu (ADMK) was 5,870 votes, or 2.88 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
