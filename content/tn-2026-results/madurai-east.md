Karthikeyan S of TVK won the Madurai East Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.74% of 271,566 votes cast. The winning margin over Moorthy P (DMK) was 16,547 votes, or 6.09 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
