Kathiravan. S of DMK won the Manachanallur Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.82% of 209,780 votes cast. The winning margin over V.Saravanan (TVK) was 12,364 votes, or 5.89 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
