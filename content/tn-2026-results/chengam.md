S.Velu of ADMK won the Chengam Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.15% of 242,897 votes cast. The winning margin over K Bharathidhasan (TVK) was 13,278 votes, or 5.47 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
