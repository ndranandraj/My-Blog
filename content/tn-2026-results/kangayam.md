Nsn Nataraj of ADMK won the Kangayam Assembly constituency (Tiruppur district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.94% of 203,534 votes cast. The winning margin over Mani Gounder (TVK) was 8,133 votes, or 4.0 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
