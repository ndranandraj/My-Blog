Arul Vignesh C of TVK won the Kallakurichi Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.5% of 249,626 votes cast. The winning margin over Rajeevgandhi S (ADMK) was 798 votes, or 0.32 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
