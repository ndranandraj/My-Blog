Shanmugam C Ve of ADMK won the Mailam Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.52% of 189,221 votes cast. The winning margin over Vijay Niranjan A (TVK) was 30,041 votes, or 15.88 percentage points. The seat **flipped from PMK to ADMK** since 2021.
