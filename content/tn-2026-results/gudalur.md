Dhravidamani.M of DMK won the Gudalur Assembly constituency (The nilgiris district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.37% of 151,241 votes cast. The winning margin over Pon.Jayaseelan (ADMK) was 22,833 votes, or 15.1 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
