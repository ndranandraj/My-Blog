S.P.Velumani of ADMK won the Thondamuthur Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.09% of 258,549 votes cast. The winning margin over Sathish Raju (Alias) K.P.R. Sathish (TVK) was 14,725 votes, or 5.7 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
