Ramesh of TVK won the Srirangam Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.49% of 254,985 votes cast. The winning margin over S. Durairaj (DMK) was 33,590 votes, or 13.17 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
