Thangapandi Sr of TVK won the Madurai West Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.78% of 216,403 votes cast. The winning margin over Balaji R (DMK) was 11,931 votes, or 5.51 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
