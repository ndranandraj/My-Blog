P K Sekarbabu of DMK won the Harbour Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.43% of 99,622 votes cast. The winning margin over Sinora P S Ashok (TVK) was 11,750 votes, or 11.79 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
