Veeramani K.C. of ADMK won the Jolarpet Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.91% of 213,042 votes cast. The winning margin over Munisamy C (TVK) was 16,083 votes, or 7.55 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
