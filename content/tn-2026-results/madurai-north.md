A.Kallanai of TVK won the Madurai North Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.03% of 161,801 votes cast. The winning margin over G.Thalapathi (DMK) was 18,038 votes, or 11.15 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
