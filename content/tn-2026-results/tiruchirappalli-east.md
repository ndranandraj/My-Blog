C. Joseph Vijay of TVK won the Tiruchirappalli (East) Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 50.07% of 182,493 votes cast. The winning margin over S. Inigo Irudayaraj (DMK) was 27,416 votes, or 15.02 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
