Dr.Thirupathi. N of TVK won the Tirupattur Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 51.79% of 202,925 votes cast. The winning margin over Nallathambi. A (DMK) was 48,263 votes, or 23.78 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
