Jayakumar. S of ADMK won the Perundurai Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.03% of 200,686 votes cast. The winning margin over Thoppu Venkatachalam (Alias) N.D. Venkatachalam (DMK) was 9,693 votes, or 4.83 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
