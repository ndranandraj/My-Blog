R. Kathiravan of TVK won the Manapparai Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.43% of 241,216 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. Pl. Vijayakumar (ADMK) was 1,426 votes, or 0.59 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
