V Senthilbalaji of DMK won the Coimbatore (South) Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.16% of 156,499 votes cast. The winning margin over V. Senthilkumar (TVK) was 2,271 votes, or 1.45 percentage points. The seat **flipped from BJP to DMK** since 2021.
