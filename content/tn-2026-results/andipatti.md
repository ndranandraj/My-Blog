Maharajan.A of DMK won the Andipatti Assembly constituency (Theni district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.2% of 217,329 votes cast. The winning margin over Pandi.V (TVK) was 9,554 votes, or 4.4 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
