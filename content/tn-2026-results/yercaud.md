Usharani. P of ADMK won the Yercaud Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.61% of 253,592 votes cast. The winning margin over Lakshmi Janarthanan. J (TVK) was 2,189 votes, or 0.86 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
