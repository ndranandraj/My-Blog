Palanisamy S of ADMK won the Tirukkoyilur Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.61% of 217,318 votes cast. The winning margin over Vijay R Baranibalaaji (TVK) was 285 votes, or 0.13 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
