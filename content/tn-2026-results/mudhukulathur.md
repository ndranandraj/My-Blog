R.S.Rajakannappan of DMK won the Mudhukulathur Assembly constituency (Ramanathapuram district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 29.39% of 231,373 votes cast. The winning margin over Malarvizhi. B (TVK) was 16,598 votes, or 7.17 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
