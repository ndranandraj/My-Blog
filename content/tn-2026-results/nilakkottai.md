Ayyanar.R of TVK won the Nilakkottai Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.71% of 197,593 votes cast. The winning margin over Nagajothi.S (DMK) was 2,925 votes, or 1.48 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
