Jayasudha. L of ADMK won the Arani Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.34% of 230,162 votes cast. The winning margin over Mahalakshmi Govarthanan (DMK) was 5,631 votes, or 2.45 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
