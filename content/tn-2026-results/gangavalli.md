Nallathambi. A of ADMK won the Gangavalli Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.2% of 196,679 votes cast. The winning margin over Chinnadurai. K (DMK) was 14,404 votes, or 7.32 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
