Ramachandran. K.K.S.S.R of DMK won the Aruppukkottai Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.49% of 178,413 votes cast. The winning margin over Karthik Kumar. K (TVK) was 4,943 votes, or 2.77 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
