Rakesh R of ADMK won the Sankarapuram Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.07% of 235,528 votes cast. The winning margin over Udhayasuriyan T (DMK) was 3,440 votes, or 1.46 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
