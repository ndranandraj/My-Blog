Rajmohan of TVK won the Egmore Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.02% of 119,738 votes cast. The winning margin over Tamilan Prasanna (DMK) was 10,804 votes, or 9.02 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
