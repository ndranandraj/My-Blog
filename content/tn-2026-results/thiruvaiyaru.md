Durai. Chandrasekaran of DMK won the Thiruvaiyaru Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.21% of 222,111 votes cast. The winning margin over Mathi. Manikandan (TVK) was 8,555 votes, or 3.85 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
