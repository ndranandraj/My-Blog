Prabhakar.J.C.D of TVK won the Thousand Lights Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.73% of 131,824 votes cast. The winning margin over Ezhilan Naganathan (DMK) was 15,141 votes, or 11.49 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
