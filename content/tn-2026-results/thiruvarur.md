Kalaivanan Poondi K of DMK won the Thiruvarur Assembly constituency (Thiruvarur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.87% of 217,875 votes cast. The winning margin over Veeramani V (TVK) was 18,148 votes, or 8.33 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
