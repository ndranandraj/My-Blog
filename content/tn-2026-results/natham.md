Natham Viswanathan R of ADMK won the Natham Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.84% of 239,140 votes cast. The winning margin over Selvakumar K (DMK) was 11,869 votes, or 4.96 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
