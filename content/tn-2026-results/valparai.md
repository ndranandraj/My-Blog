Kutty (Alias) Sudhakar. A of DMK won the Valparai Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.34% of 150,431 votes cast. The winning margin over Dr. Sridharan. A (TVK) was 9,371 votes, or 6.23 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
