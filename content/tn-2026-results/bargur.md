E.C. Govindarasan of ADMK won the Bargur Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.3% of 213,960 votes cast. The winning margin over E. Muralidharan (TVK) was 4,241 votes, or 1.98 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
