D.Velazhagan of ADMK won the Anaikattu Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.27% of 216,318 votes cast. The winning margin over A.P.Nandakumar (DMK) was 7,081 votes, or 3.27 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
