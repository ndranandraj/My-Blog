Marimuthu.K of CPI won the Thiruthuraipoondi Assembly constituency (Thiruvarur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.77% of 191,030 votes cast. The winning margin over Pandiyan.S (TVK) was 12,922 votes, or 6.76 percentage points. CPI held the seat it won here in 2021.
