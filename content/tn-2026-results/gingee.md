Ganeshkumar A of PMK won the Gingee Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.1% of 216,652 votes cast. The winning margin over Gingee Masthan K S (DMK) was 12,645 votes, or 5.84 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to PMK** since 2021.
