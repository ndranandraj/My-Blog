Senthil Kumar. N of TVK won the Thiruvottiyur Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 53.15% of 207,088 votes cast. The winning margin over Sundararaj. L (CPI(M)) was 53,564 votes, or 25.87 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
