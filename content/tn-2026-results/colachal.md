Tharahai Cuthbert of INC won the Colachal Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.27% of 199,013 votes cast. The winning margin over Prem Alex Lawrence (TVK) was 2,833 votes, or 1.42 percentage points. INC held the seat it won here in 2021.
