R.V. Ranjithkumar of TVK won the Kancheepuram Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.09% of 246,317 votes cast. The winning margin over V. Somasundaram (ADMK) was 15,488 votes, or 6.29 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
