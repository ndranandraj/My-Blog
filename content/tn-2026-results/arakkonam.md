V. Gandhiraj of TVK won the Arakkonam Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.46% of 182,340 votes cast. The winning margin over Ezhil Caroline (VCK) was 23,121 votes, or 12.68 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
