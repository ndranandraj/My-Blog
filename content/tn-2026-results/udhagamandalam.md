Bhojarajan.M of BJP won the Udhagamandalam Assembly constituency (The nilgiris district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.61% of 148,704 votes cast. The winning margin over Ibrahim.R (TVK) was 976 votes, or 0.66 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to BJP** since 2021.
