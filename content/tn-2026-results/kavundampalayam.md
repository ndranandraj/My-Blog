Kanimozhi Santhosh of TVK won the Kavundampalayam Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.24% of 363,991 votes cast. The winning margin over Arunkumar Prg (ADMK) was 42,140 votes, or 11.58 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
