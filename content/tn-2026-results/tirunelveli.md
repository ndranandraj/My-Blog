Murughan.R.S. of TVK won the Tirunelveli Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.72% of 218,425 votes cast. The winning margin over Subramanian.S (DMK) was 11,414 votes, or 5.23 percentage points. The seat **flipped from BJP to TVK** since 2021.
