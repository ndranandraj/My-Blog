Srinath of TVK won the Thoothukkudi Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 49.11% of 204,712 votes cast. The winning margin over P. Geetha Jeevan (DMK) was 37,731 votes, or 18.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
