Sowmiya Anbumani of PMK won the Dharmapuri Assembly constituency (Dharmapuri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.28% of 238,579 votes cast. The winning margin over Sivan. M. (TVK) was 20,896 votes, or 8.76 percentage points. PMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
