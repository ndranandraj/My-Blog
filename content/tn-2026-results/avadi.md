R.Ramesh Kumar of TVK won the Avadi Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 52.13% of 346,005 votes cast. The winning margin over S.M.Nasar (DMK) was 76,311 votes, or 22.05 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
