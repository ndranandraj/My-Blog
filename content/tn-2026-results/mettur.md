Venkatachalam. G of ADMK won the Mettur Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.43% of 231,105 votes cast. The winning margin over Midhun Chakravarthy. M (DMK) was 19,105 votes, or 8.27 percentage points. The seat **flipped from PMK to ADMK** since 2021.
