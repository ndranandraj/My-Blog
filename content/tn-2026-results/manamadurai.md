Elangovan.D of TVK won the Manamadurai Assembly constituency (Sivaganga district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.54% of 208,643 votes cast. The winning margin over Tamilarasi.A (DMK) was 1,208 votes, or 0.58 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
