Lakshmanan.S of TVK won the Salem (West) Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 51.37% of 234,407 votes cast. The winning margin over Karthe.M (PMK) was 74,867 votes, or 31.94 percentage points. The seat **flipped from PMK to TVK** since 2021.
