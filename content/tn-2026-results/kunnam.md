Sivasankar. S.S of DMK won the Kunnam Assembly constituency (Perambalur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.84% of 230,570 votes cast. The winning margin over Saranya. A (ADMK) was 15,557 votes, or 6.75 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
