Srinivasan.P.S of DMK won the Veppanahalli Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.55% of 222,608 votes cast. The winning margin over Munusamy.K.P (ADMK) was 138 votes, or 0.06 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
