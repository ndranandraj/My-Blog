B.Vijayaraj of TVK won the Thiruporur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.96% of 262,409 votes cast. The winning margin over R. Panneerdoss (VCK) was 39,351 votes, or 15.0 percentage points. The seat **flipped from VCK to TVK** since 2021.
