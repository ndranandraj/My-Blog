Anand N of TVK won the T Nagar Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.55% of 133,928 votes cast. The winning margin over Sathiyanarayanan B (ADMK) was 13,027 votes, or 9.73 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
