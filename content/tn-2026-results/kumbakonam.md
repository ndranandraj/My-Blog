Vinoth of TVK won the Kumbakonam Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.76% of 208,288 votes cast. The winning margin over Anbalagan G (DMK) was 679 votes, or 0.33 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
