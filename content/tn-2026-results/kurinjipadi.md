M.R.K.Panneerselvam of DMK won the Kurinjipadi Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.84% of 213,965 votes cast. The winning margin over A Bhuvanenthiran (ADMK) was 7,589 votes, or 3.55 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
