Sivakumar. K of TVK won the Salem (North) Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.68% of 210,712 votes cast. The winning margin over Rajendran. R (DMK) was 14,034 votes, or 6.66 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
