Nm.Sukumar of TVK won the Sulur Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.1% of 273,544 votes cast. The winning margin over V.P.Kandasamy (ADMK) was 4,790 votes, or 1.75 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
