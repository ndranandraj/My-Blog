M.H.Jawahirullah of DMK won the Nagapattinam Assembly constituency (Nagapattinam district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.21% of 151,326 votes cast. The winning margin over M.Sugumar (TVK) was 9,781 votes, or 6.46 percentage points. The seat **flipped from VCK to DMK** since 2021.
