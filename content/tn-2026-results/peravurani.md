Ashokkumar. N of DMK won the Peravurani Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.56% of 176,274 votes cast. The winning margin over Govi.Elango (ADMK) was 3,162 votes, or 1.79 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
