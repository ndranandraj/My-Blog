E. Raja of DMK won the Vasudevanallur Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.22% of 189,795 votes cast. The winning margin over Ananthan Ayyasamy (BJP) was 6,583 votes, or 3.47 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
