Abishek. R of TVK won the Polur Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.1% of 211,743 votes cast. The winning margin over Saravanan. P (DMDK) was 227 votes, or 0.11 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
