Sivakumar C of PMK won the Vikravandi Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.33% of 209,231 votes cast. The winning margin over Vijai Vadivel A (TVK) was 910 votes, or 0.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to PMK** since 2021.
