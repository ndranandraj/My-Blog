Rajendran.R of ADMK won the Neyveli Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.76% of 168,780 votes cast. The winning margin over Saba.Rajendran (DMK) was 10,962 votes, or 6.49 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
