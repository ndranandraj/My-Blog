Latha. T of CPI(M) won the Kilvelur Assembly constituency (Nagapattinam district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.0% of 151,637 votes cast. The winning margin over Senthil Pandian. P (TVK) was 2,278 votes, or 1.5 percentage points. The seat **flipped from CPM to CPI(M)** since 2021.
