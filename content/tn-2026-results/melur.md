P.Viswanathan of INC won the Melur Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.5% of 190,726 votes cast. The winning margin over A.Maduraiveeran (TVK) was 2,724 votes, or 1.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to INC** since 2021.
