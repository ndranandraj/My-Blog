Reddiarpatti V. Narayanan of TVK won the Nanguneri Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.79% of 209,419 votes cast. The winning margin over Ruby.R.Manoharan (INC) was 16,419 votes, or 7.84 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
