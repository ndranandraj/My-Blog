Elango. R of DMK won the Aravakurichi Assembly constituency (Karur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.11% of 181,078 votes cast. The winning margin over Karthikeyan. P (TVK) was 19,382 votes, or 10.7 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
