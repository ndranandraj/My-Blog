P .Mathanraja of TVK won the Ottapidaram Assembly constituency (Thoothukkudi district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.62% of 196,117 votes cast. The winning margin over P.M.Ramajeyam (DMK) was 29,083 votes, or 14.83 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
