Arul Prakasam. M of TVK won the Saidapet Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 50.63% of 160,401 votes cast. The winning margin over Subramanian. Ma (DMK) was 28,514 votes, or 17.78 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
