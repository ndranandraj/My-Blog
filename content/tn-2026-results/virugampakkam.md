Sabarinathan.R of TVK won the Virugampakkam Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.03% of 172,831 votes cast. The winning margin over Prabhakara Raja.A.M.V (DMK) was 27,086 votes, or 15.67 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
