N. Subramanian of TVK won the Gandarvakkottai Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.08% of 167,618 votes cast. The winning margin over C. Uthayakumar (BJP) was 11,039 votes, or 6.59 percentage points. The seat **flipped from CPM to TVK** since 2021.
