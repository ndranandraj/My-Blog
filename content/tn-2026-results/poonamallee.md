Prakasam.R of TVK won the Poonamallee Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 52.22% of 308,894 votes cast. The winning margin over Krishnaswamy.A (DMK) was 72,740 votes, or 23.55 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
