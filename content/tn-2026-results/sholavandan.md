Karuppaiah.M.V of TVK won the Sholavandan Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.8% of 189,080 votes cast. The winning margin over Venkatesan.A (DMK) was 2,678 votes, or 1.42 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
