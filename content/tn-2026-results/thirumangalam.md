Manimaran.M of DMK won the Thirumangalam Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.22% of 230,980 votes cast. The winning margin over Udhayakumar.R.B (ADMK) was 23,807 votes, or 10.31 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
