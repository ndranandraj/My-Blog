Gowtham D of DMK won the Vanur Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.85% of 192,119 votes cast. The winning margin over Suresh P (TVK) was 7,034 votes, or 3.66 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to DMK** since 2021.
