Sekar S of ADMK won the Paramathi-Velur Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.49% of 188,845 votes cast. The winning margin over Moorthiy K S (DMK) was 308 votes, or 0.16 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
