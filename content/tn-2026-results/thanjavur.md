R. Vijaysaravanan of TVK won the Thanjavur Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.12% of 198,798 votes cast. The winning margin over Shan. Ramanathan (DMK) was 16,955 votes, or 8.53 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
