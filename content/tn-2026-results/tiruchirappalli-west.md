K.N.Nehru of DMK won the Tiruchirappalli (West) Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.94% of 196,347 votes cast. The winning margin over G.Ramamoorthi (TVK) was 4,786 votes, or 2.44 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
