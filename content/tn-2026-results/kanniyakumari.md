Thalavai Sundaram. N of ADMK won the Kanniyakumari Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.38% of 239,150 votes cast. The winning margin over Mahesh.R (DMK) was 214 votes, or 0.09 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
