Anbalagan. K.P. of ADMK won the Palacodu Assembly constituency (Dharmapuri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.64% of 225,257 votes cast. The winning margin over Gopi. R. (TVK) was 39,042 votes, or 17.33 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
