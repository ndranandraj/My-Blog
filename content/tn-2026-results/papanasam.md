A.M. Shahjahan of IUML won the Papanasam Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.35% of 207,723 votes cast. The winning margin over Azarudeen Uduman Ali (TVK) was 1,065 votes, or 0.51 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to IUML** since 2021.
