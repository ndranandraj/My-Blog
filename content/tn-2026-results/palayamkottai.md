M.Abdul Wahab of DMK won the Palayamkottai Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.15% of 180,620 votes cast. The winning margin over Maria John (TVK) was 13,805 votes, or 7.64 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
