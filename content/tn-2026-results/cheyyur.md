Rajasekar. E of ADMK won the Cheyyur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.02% of 187,584 votes cast. The winning margin over Mohan Raja.M.G.K (TVK) was 5,668 votes, or 3.02 percentage points. The seat **flipped from VCK to ADMK** since 2021.
