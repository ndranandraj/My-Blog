Sampathkumar. V of ADMK won the Harur Assembly constituency (Dharmapuri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.32% of 220,042 votes cast. The winning margin over Shanmugam. A (DMK) was 3,329 votes, or 1.51 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
