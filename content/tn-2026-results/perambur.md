C. Joseph Vijay of TVK won the Perambur Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 58.89% of 204,384 votes cast. The winning margin over R.D. Shekar (DMK) was 53,715 votes, or 26.28 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
