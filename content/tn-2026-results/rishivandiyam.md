Karthikeyan K of DMK won the Rishivandiyam Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.65% of 244,795 votes cast. The winning margin over Ashok Kumar G (TVK) was 4,862 votes, or 1.99 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
