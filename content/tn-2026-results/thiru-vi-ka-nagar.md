M. R. Pallavi of TVK won the Thiru-Vi-Ka-Nagar Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 48.04% of 143,903 votes cast. The winning margin over K. S. Ravichandran (DMK) was 22,333 votes, or 15.52 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
