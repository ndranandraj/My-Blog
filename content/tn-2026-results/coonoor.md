M. Raju of DMK won the Coonoor Assembly constituency (The nilgiris district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.69% of 141,394 votes cast. The winning margin over A. Ramu (ADMK) was 8,099 votes, or 5.73 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
