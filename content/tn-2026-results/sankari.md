Vetrivel. S of ADMK won the Sankari Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.83% of 243,779 votes cast. The winning margin over Senthilkumar. K (TVK) was 9,517 votes, or 3.9 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
