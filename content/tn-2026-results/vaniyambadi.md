Syed Farooq Basha Ssb of IUML won the Vaniyambadi Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.43% of 212,534 votes cast. The winning margin over Syed Bhurhanudeen (TVK) was 2,982 votes, or 1.4 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to IUML** since 2021.
