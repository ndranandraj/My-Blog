L.E. Jothimani of VCK won the Kattumannarkoil Assembly constituency (Cuddalore district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 45.33% of 187,905 votes cast. The winning margin over A. Sozhan (PMK) was 33,063 votes, or 17.6 percentage points. VCK held the seat it won here in 2021.
