V.K.Ramkumar of TVK won the Anna Nagar Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 43.7% of 163,339 votes cast. The winning margin over N.Chitrarasu (DMK) was 21,363 votes, or 13.08 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
