Kadarkarairaj. A of DMK won the Sattur Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 31.23% of 198,734 votes cast. The winning margin over Nainar Nagenthran (BJP) was 5,989 votes, or 3.01 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
