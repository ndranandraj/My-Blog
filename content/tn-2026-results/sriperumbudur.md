Thennarasu.K of TVK won the Sriperumbudur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.58% of 331,088 votes cast. The winning margin over K. Selvaperunthagai (INC) was 54,246 votes, or 16.38 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
