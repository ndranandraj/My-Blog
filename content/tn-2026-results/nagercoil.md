Austin of DMK won the Nagercoil Assembly constituency (Kanniyakumari district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.6% of 196,280 votes cast. The winning margin over Bervin Kings G (TVK) was 7,570 votes, or 3.86 percentage points. The seat **flipped from BJP to DMK** since 2021.
