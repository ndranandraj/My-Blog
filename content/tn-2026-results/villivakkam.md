Aadhav Arjuna of TVK won the Villivakkam Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 46.97% of 141,452 votes cast. The winning margin over Karthik Mohan (DMK) was 17,302 votes, or 12.23 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
