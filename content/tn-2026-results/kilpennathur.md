Ramachandran.S of ADMK won the Kilpennathur Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.18% of 219,790 votes cast. The winning margin over Pitchandi.K (DMK) was 30,465 votes, or 13.86 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
