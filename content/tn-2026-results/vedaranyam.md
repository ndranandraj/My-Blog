Manian. O.S of ADMK won the Vedaranyam Assembly constituency (Nagapattinam district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.48% of 162,216 votes cast. The winning margin over Pugazhendi. M (DMK) was 7,331 votes, or 4.52 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
