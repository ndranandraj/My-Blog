M.L.Vijayprabhu of TVK won the Madavaram Assembly constituency (Thiruvallur district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 52.61% of 361,998 votes cast. The winning margin over S.Sudharsanam (DMK) was 94,985 votes, or 26.24 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
