Karuppanan. K.C of ADMK won the Bhavani Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.28% of 208,323 votes cast. The winning margin over Balakrishnan (TVK) was 7,396 votes, or 3.55 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
