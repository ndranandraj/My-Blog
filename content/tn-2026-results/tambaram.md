D.Sarathkumar of TVK won the Tambaram Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.67% of 278,809 votes cast. The winning margin over R.S.Kiruthika Devi (DMK) was 35,621 votes, or 12.78 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
