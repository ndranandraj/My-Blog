Jeganathmishra Pla of TVK won the Cumbum Assembly constituency (Theni district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.55% of 210,574 votes cast. The winning margin over Eramakrishnan N (DMK) was 751 votes, or 0.36 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
