Siva.V.Meyyanathan of DMK won the Alangudi Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.98% of 180,483 votes cast. The winning margin over Kandasamy (TVK) was 12,977 votes, or 7.19 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
