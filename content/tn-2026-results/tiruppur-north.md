V.Sathyabama of TVK won the Tiruppur (North) Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 49.88% of 263,457 votes cast. The winning margin over M.S.M.Anandan (ADMK) was 69,992 votes, or 26.57 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
