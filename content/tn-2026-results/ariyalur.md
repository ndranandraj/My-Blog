Rajendran S of ADMK won the Ariyalur Assembly constituency (Ariyalur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.37% of 235,858 votes cast. The winning margin over Latha Balu (DMK) was 24,498 votes, or 10.39 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
