Ecr P Saravanan of TVK won the Shozhinganallur Assembly constituency (Kancheepuram district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 49.27% of 447,295 votes cast. The winning margin over S. Aravind Ramesh (DMK) was 96,780 votes, or 21.64 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
