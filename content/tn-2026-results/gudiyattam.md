K.Sindu of TVK won the Gudiyattam Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.17% of 229,076 votes cast. The winning margin over K.B.Pradap (DMDK) was 10,097 votes, or 4.41 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
