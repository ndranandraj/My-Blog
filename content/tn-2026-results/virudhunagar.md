Selvam P of TVK won the Virudhunagar Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.93% of 167,832 votes cast. The winning margin over Vijayaprabhakar (DMDK) was 9,391 votes, or 5.6 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
