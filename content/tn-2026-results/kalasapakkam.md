Agri Krishnamurthy. S S of ADMK won the Kalasapakkam Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.86% of 214,127 votes cast. The winning margin over Saravanan. P.S.T (DMK) was 26,740 votes, or 12.49 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
