Balakrishnareddy. P of ADMK won the Hosur Assembly constituency (Krishnagiri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.55% of 277,770 votes cast. The winning margin over Vendarkarasan.S (TVK) was 27,803 votes, or 10.01 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
