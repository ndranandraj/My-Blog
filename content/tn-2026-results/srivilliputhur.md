Karthik.A of TVK won the Srivilliputhur Assembly constituency (Virudhunagar district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.93% of 193,471 votes cast. The winning margin over Mahalingam.P (CPI) was 8,581 votes, or 4.44 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
