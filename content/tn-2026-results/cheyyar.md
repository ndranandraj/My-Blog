Mukkur N. Subramanian of ADMK won the Cheyyar Assembly constituency (Tiruvannamalai district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.95% of 222,570 votes cast. The winning margin over Dusi K Mohan (TVK) was 21,081 votes, or 9.47 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
