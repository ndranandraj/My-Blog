I. Periasamy of DMK won the Athoor Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.79% of 237,183 votes cast. The winning margin over N. Kalaiselvi (TVK) was 22,368 votes, or 9.43 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
