Mani. R of ADMK won the Omalur Assembly constituency (Salem district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 41.4% of 271,119 votes cast. The winning margin over Adhiyamaan. R.V (TVK) was 14,539 votes, or 5.36 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
