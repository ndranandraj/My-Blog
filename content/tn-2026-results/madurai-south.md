M.M.Gopison of TVK won the Madurai South Assembly constituency (Madurai district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.11% of 141,503 votes cast. The winning margin over M.Boominathan (DMK) was 21,529 votes, or 15.21 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
