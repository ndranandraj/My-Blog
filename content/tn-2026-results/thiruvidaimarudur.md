Govi.Chezhiaan of DMK won the Thiruvidaimarudur Assembly constituency (Thanjavur district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 38.26% of 208,944 votes cast. The winning margin over S.Prabakaran (TVK) was 14,116 votes, or 6.76 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
