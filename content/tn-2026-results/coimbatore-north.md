V. Sampathkumar of TVK won the Coimbatore (North) Assembly constituency (Coimbatore district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.91% of 226,109 votes cast. The winning margin over Durai. Senthamil Selvan B.E (DMK) was 21,992 votes, or 9.73 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
