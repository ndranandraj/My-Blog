K.Ramkumar of TVK won the Palladam Assembly constituency (Tiruppur district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 40.3% of 301,014 votes cast. The winning margin over K.P.Paramasivam (ADMK) was 37,897 votes, or 12.59 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
