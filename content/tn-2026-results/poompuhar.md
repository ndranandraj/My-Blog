Nivedha M Murugan of DMK won the Poompuhar Assembly constituency (Nagapattinam district, Cauvery Delta) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.98% of 219,324 votes cast. The winning margin over Pavunraj. S (ADMK) was 8,260 votes, or 3.77 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
