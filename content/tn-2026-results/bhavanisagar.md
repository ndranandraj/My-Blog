V.P.Tamilselvi of TVK won the Bhavanisagar Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 33.73% of 214,595 votes cast. The winning margin over A.Bannari (ADMK) was 4,569 votes, or 2.13 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
