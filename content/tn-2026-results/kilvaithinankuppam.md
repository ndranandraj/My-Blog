Thenral Kumar. E of TVK won the Kilvaithinankuppam Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 39.13% of 189,892 votes cast. The winning margin over Jegan Moorthy. M (ADMK) was 20,255 votes, or 10.67 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
