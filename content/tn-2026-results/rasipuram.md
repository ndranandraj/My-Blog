Logesh Tamilselvan D of TVK won the Rasipuram Assembly constituency (Namakkal district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.55% of 204,680 votes cast. The winning margin over Premkumar S D (BJP) was 14,511 votes, or 7.09 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
