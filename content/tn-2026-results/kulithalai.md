Suriyanur. A. Chandran of DMK won the Kulithalai Assembly constituency (Karur district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.59% of 209,091 votes cast. The winning margin over G.Balasubramani (TVK) was 579 votes, or 0.28 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
