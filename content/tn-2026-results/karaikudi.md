Dr.Prabhu. Tk of TVK won the Karaikudi Assembly constituency (Sivaganga district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 44.21% of 229,267 votes cast. The winning margin over Mangudi. S (INC) was 46,074 votes, or 20.1 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
