Dr.Sathish Christopher of TVK won the Radhapuram Assembly constituency (Tirunelveli district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.99% of 199,931 votes cast. The winning margin over Appavu.M (DMK) was 12,313 votes, or 6.16 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
