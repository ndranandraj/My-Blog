Gajendran. S. of TVK won the Pennagaram Assembly constituency (Dharmapuri district, Krishnagiri Belt) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 35.53% of 228,666 votes cast. The winning margin over Selvam. V. (PMK) was 3,165 votes, or 1.38 percentage points. The seat **flipped from PMK to TVK** since 2021.
