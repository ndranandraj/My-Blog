Vijayakumar (A) Navalpattu S. Viji of TVK won the Thiruverumbur Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 42.06% of 213,583 votes cast. The winning margin over Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi (DMK) was 8,705 votes, or 4.08 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
