G.Kapil of TVK won the Sholinghur Assembly constituency (Vellore district, North) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.16% of 233,684 votes cast. The winning margin over K.Saravanan (PMK) was 5,686 votes, or 2.43 percentage points. The seat **flipped from INC to TVK** since 2021.
