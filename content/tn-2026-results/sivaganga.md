Kulanthai Rani A of TVK won the Sivaganga Assembly constituency (Sivaganga district, Deep South) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 34.45% of 214,067 votes cast. The winning margin over Senthilnathan Pr (ADMK) was 15,081 votes, or 7.04 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
