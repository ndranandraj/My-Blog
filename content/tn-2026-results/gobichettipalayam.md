Sengottaiyan.K.A of TVK won the Gobichettipalayam Assembly constituency (Erode district, Kongu (West)) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 37.6% of 219,727 votes cast. The winning margin over Nallasivam.N (DMK) was 16,620 votes, or 7.56 percentage points. The seat **flipped from ADMK to TVK** since 2021.
