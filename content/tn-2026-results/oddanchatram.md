Sakkarapani. R of DMK won the Oddanchatram Assembly constituency (Dindigul district, Madurai Region) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 46.17% of 201,624 votes cast. The winning margin over Vidiyal S. Sekar (BJP) was 43,249 votes, or 21.45 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
