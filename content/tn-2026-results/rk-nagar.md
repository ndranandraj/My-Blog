N. Marie Wilson of TVK won the Rk Nagar Assembly constituency (Chennai district, Chennai & Suburbs) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 53.97% of 181,206 votes cast. The winning margin over J. John Ebenezer (DMK) was 49,668 votes, or 27.41 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to TVK** since 2021.
