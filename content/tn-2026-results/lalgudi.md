Leemarose Martin of ADMK won the Lalgudi Assembly constituency (Tiruchirappalli district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 32.82% of 185,213 votes cast. The winning margin over Ku Pa Krishnan (TVK) was 2,739 votes, or 1.48 percentage points. The seat **flipped from DMK to ADMK** since 2021.
