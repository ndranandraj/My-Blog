Vijayabaskar. C of ADMK won the Viralimalai Assembly constituency (Pudukkottai district, Central) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 51.87% of 203,921 votes cast. The winning margin over Murugesan. P (TVK) was 62,073 votes, or 30.44 percentage points. ADMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
