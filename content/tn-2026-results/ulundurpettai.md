Vasanthavel G R of DMK won the Ulundurpettai Assembly constituency (Viluppuram district, Northeast Coast) in the Tamil Nadu 2026 election, taking 36.34% of 271,002 votes cast. The winning margin over Kumaraguru R (ADMK) was 2,277 votes, or 0.84 percentage points. DMK held the seat it won here in 2021.
