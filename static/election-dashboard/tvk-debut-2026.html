The Edappadi exception

Of all 234 constituencies, the one TVK chose not to contest is AC #86 — Edappadi. Sitting AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami's home seat. Vijay's party fielded a candidate against every other major-party leader in TN. Not against EPS.

Punching above weight: Chennai & Suburbs (+18.5 seats), Kongu (+8.4), Madurai region (+3.6). Punching below weight: Cauvery Delta (-3.5), Northeast Coast (-3.9). The first three are where the wave broke loose. The last two are DMK fortresses that absorbed it.

Seat-vs-vote efficiency, all parties

The cleanest single number for understanding how TVK's 34.9% vote share converted to 46.2% of seats. Efficiency is seat share divided by vote share. Above 1.0 means the party converted votes to seats better than uniform; below 1.0 means worse. NTK at zero is the "votes that elected nobody" story; BJP at 0.14 is the spread-thin story.
