The Edappadi exception
TVK filed a candidate against every major AIADMK leader in TN 2026 — except one. AC #88 Edappadi, home constituency of AIADMK general secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami, was left uncontested. The only blank in 234 constituencies. Read as strategic deference, or a calculation that EPS in his fortress was not a fight worth picking.
