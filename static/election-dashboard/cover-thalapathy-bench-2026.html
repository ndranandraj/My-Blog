The fan club, in office
Tamil Nadu's fan club captains have always borrowed a star's name as a badge. In my Thiruvarur years, two of the most popular young men in the neighbourhood went by Rajini Sakthi and Rajini Chinna. Neither was born with that name. What TVK has done in 2026 is the natural next chapter: the rasigar mandram captains have walked into the legislature, the founder's name still pinned to their own. Eleven of TVK's twelve Vijays are namesakes. One is the man himself, and he won two seats.
